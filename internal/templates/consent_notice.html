What data we use

We use the account identity and contact details you provide: your name, email address, and phone number.

Why we use it

We use this data only to create and operate your account on this platform — signing you in, running your workspace, and providing the transport operations features in it. We call this purpose "platform use".

Your choice

Giving consent is voluntary. But without it we cannot create or operate your account.

Withdrawing your consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time — with the "Withdraw consent" button below, or by sending POST /api/v1/consent/withdraw while signed in. If you withdraw, you will not be able to log in until you grant consent again.

Questions or complaints

For questions or grievances about your personal data, contact us from the Contact page (/contact-us) and we will respond.
