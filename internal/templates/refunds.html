Refund Policy

Effective: 30 July 2026  |  Version: V1.0

1. Eligibility

We issue a refund only where the service has not been provided by the Company. Where a service is delivered, no refund is due.

2. Submission Window

A valid refund request must be submitted within the time window prescribed for the relevant service or as set out in our policies. Any request submitted beyond the prescribed timeframe is deemed ineligible, and no refund will be processed thereafter.

3. Processing

Refunds are processed in accordance with the timelines and conditions applicable to the specific service availed, subject to verification that the service was not delivered. Approved refunds are processed through the original payment channel (e.g., Razorpay) and may take several business days to reflect, depending on your bank or provider.

4. Non-Refundable Items

  • Fees for services already rendered or consumed;
  • Prepaid subscription or plan portions used or partially used, except where the service was not delivered;
  • Third-party charges (e.g., telecom, gateway) that are non-reversible.

5. How to Request

Raise a request through your account or by contacting support. Include your invoice or reference number and the reason for the request. See also our Terms of Service (Cancellation & Refund).
