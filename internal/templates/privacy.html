Privacy Policy

Effective: 30 July 2026  |  Version: V2.0  |  Governed by the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

Data Fiduciary: Avandab Operations Platform (“we”, “our”) determines the purpose and means of processing personal data and is accountable for compliance with the DPDP Act, 2023.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Drivers: real-time and background location, electronic proof-of-delivery (EPOD) photos, device identifier, call logs, camera/photo library, attendance records.
  • Customers (corporate shippers): company name, GST number, billing address, payment terms, invoice and credit-balance records.
  • Device & app data: operating system, hardware model, unique device identifier, mobile network, preferred language.
  • Website visitors: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and (with consent) analytics data.

2. Purpose of Processing

  • Providing fleet & logistics management services;
  • Account creation and authentication;
  • Vehicle tracking and logistics operations;
  • Customer support and dispute resolution;
  • Billing and payment processing;
  • Regulatory compliance and fraud prevention;
  • Service improvement and analytics (with consent).

3. Legal Basis (DPDP Act, 2023)

We process personal data:

  • with the consent of the Data Principal (free, specific, informed, unambiguous, via clear affirmative action); or
  • for the legitimate uses permitted under Section 7 of the DPDP Act — including voluntary data for a specified purpose (where you have not indicated non-consent), compliance with Indian law or judgments, responding to a medical emergency, measures during an epidemic or disaster, and employment safeguards.

Where personal data is necessary to provide a service, failure to provide it may mean we cannot deliver that service or feature.

4. Consent & Withdrawal

You may withdraw consent at any time, and the process to withdraw is as easy as the process to give it. On withdrawal we, and any processor engaged by us, will cease processing the relevant data within a reasonable time and erase it, unless retention is required by law. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

5. Use & Disclosure

We share personal data only with authorised service providers (cloud hosting, payment processors, communication & support platforms, analytics, professional advisers, auditors) under contractual obligations requiring appropriate security safeguards and processing solely on our documented instructions. We do not sell personal data. Disclosure may also occur to comply with law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and property.

6. International Transfers

Personal data may be transferred outside India only in accordance with Section 16 of the DPDP Act and any Central Government restrictions. Primary storage is in India; where AI-enabled features are used, limited data may be processed by authorised providers under contract.

7. Security & Retention

We implement reasonable technical and organisational safeguards — encryption, access controls, logging, backups — to protect personal data. Data is retained only as long as necessary for the purpose, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce rights, after which it is securely erased or anonymised.

8. Personal Data Breach

On becoming aware of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India without delay and, where required, notify affected Data Principals in clear language describing the breach, its consequences, and the measures taken.

9. Children

Our services are directed to adults. We do not process the personal data of a person below 18 years without verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian.

10. Cookies & Tracking

We use essential cookies (session, authentication, security) that are required for the site to function and do not require consent. We use non-essential cookies (analytics such as PostHog) only after you accept them via the consent banner. You can change your choice at any time through Cookie Settings or by clearing site data in your browser.

11. Your Rights (DPDP Act, Sections 11–15)

  • Obtain information about the processing of your personal data;
  • Request correction, completion, updating or erasure;
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights on death or incapacity;
  • Request a summary of the personal data being processed;
  • Seek grievance redressal and, if unresolved, approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Requests may be made to the Grievance Officer (details below). We acknowledge within 3 business days and respond within 30 days.

12. Grievance Officer (DPDP Act, Section 5(8))

  • Name: [Finance & Legal Lead]
  • Designation: Grievance Officer
  • Email: grievance@avandab.com
  • Address: [Registered office, New Delhi, India]

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Effective” date above. Continued use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
