Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions every fleet owner asks. Still stuck? Contact support.

How long does setup take?

Most fleets are operational in about 10 minutes: register, onboard your company, add vehicles and drivers, and create your first booking.

How does trip tracking work?

Trips move through scheduled, assigned, in-transit and delivered states. Vehicles with GPS hardware report positions live; customers can follow a share link without logging in.

What is ePOD and how is delivery verified?

Electronic proof of delivery: the driver uploads a photo or PDF at delivery, optionally verified with a one-time password shared with the consignee.

Can my customers track their shipments?

Yes. Grant a customer portal access and they see only their own bookings, invoices and trip tracking. Public share links work without login.

Does Avandab handle billing and GST?

Completed trips generate invoices automatically, including GST breakdown with e-invoice (IRN/QR) support, and payments are recorded against them.

Is my data isolated from other companies?

Yes. Every record is tenant-scoped and enforced at the middleware layer; users only ever see their own organisation's data.

How is my data protected?

Data is protected under India's DPDP Act, 2023. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why, and contact us to exercise your rights.

What GPS hardware is supported?

Standard GPS trackers that report over TCP, plus manual trip updates and GPX file import for vehicles without live hardware.

Is there a driver mobile app?

Yes. Drivers get trip assignments, navigation, delivery verification and expense logging, with offline support that syncs later.

How do I get support?

Use the contact page to raise a ticket and track its status, or reply from your account for billing and operations help.
