Frequently Asked Questions

Are there hidden device-integration or telemetry ingestion fees?

No. We support standard AIS-140 GPS trackers, OBD/CAN devices, and temperature probes without per-packet or ingestion markup fees.

How does the 14-day free trial work?

You receive complete access to GPS tracking, driver dispatch, and ePOD for up to 5 vehicles. At the end of 14 days, you can choose to activate a paid tier or export your trip records.

Where is our fleet and driver data stored?

In accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023), all fleet records, telemetry logs, and driver personal data are stored in certified secure data centers within India.
