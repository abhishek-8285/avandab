Consumer Compliance & Dark-Pattern Certificate

Effective: 15 September 2026  |  Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 (eff. 1 Jan 2027) · Dark Patterns Guidelines, 2023

Self-audit certificate: Avandab Operations Platform has reviewed its signup, booking-quote, checkout (Razorpay), invoice and consent flows against the 13 dark patterns in the Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023 and found no false urgency, basket sneaking, confirm shaming, forced action, subscription trap, interface interference, bait-and-switch, drip pricing, disguised advertisement, nagging, trick questions, SaaS-billing traps or rogue malware. Re-audited yearly; this certificate is displayed prominently per Rule 4(15).

1. Who we are

Avandab Operations Platform, New Delhi, India. Customer care: support@avandab.com · +91 8285336507. Grievance Officer (Finance & Legal): grievance@avandab.com · +91 8285336507 (Mon–Sat). Full registered-office details on request via Contact Us.

2. Grievance redressal

Acknowledged within 48 hours with a copy of the recorded complaint (see ticket copy/print on Contact Us), redressed within 1 month. Escalate to the Grievance Officer, then the National Consumer Helpline (NCH) convergence process. Data-privacy requests follow DPDP timelines (ack 3 business days, response 30 days) — see Privacy Policy.

3. Pricing, discounts & fees

Totals shown upfront with GST breakup; no drip pricing or bundled unrelated fees. Any future plan/subscription discount shows reduced + prior price (prior = lowest price in preceding 30 days). Consent is explicit and affirmative — no pre-ticked boxes; withdrawal as easy as giving (see /consent). Refunds: 30-day request window, 5–7 business-day processing via original channel — see Refund Policy.

4. Scope note

Avandab is a first-party B2B fleet SaaS (own plans + freight bookings), not a marketplace and holds no goods inventory. No consumer product search ranking, sponsored listings, third-party seller registry, country-of-origin/importer labels or best-before dates apply. If third-party sellers or public search/ads launch, ranking factors and sponsored labels will be disclosed here first.
