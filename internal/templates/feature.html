How long does setup take?
Most teams are running in the same day. Add your vehicles and drivers, and start creating trips — no hardware or training required to begin.
Is my data secure?
Data is encrypted in transit and at rest (AES-256), access is role-based, and every meaningful action is recorded in append-only audit logs. Data is hosted in India.
Do drivers need a smartphone?
The web app works on any device with a browser. Drivers can use the mobile app for e-POD and status updates from any Android phone.
Is this actually GST-compliant?
Invoices carry your company's GSTIN and tax details. Line items follow GST structure so your accountant can file without reformatting. CGST/SGST for in-state, IGST for inter-state.
Can we start with just 5–10 vehicles?
Avandab scales from a handful of vehicles to hundreds. Start with a pilot, prove the value, and expand — no long-term contracts required.
